## Problem

Installing an app just to blow off steam is a hassle. I wanted something you open in one tab and feel better in 30 seconds.

## Solution

- Wrote a PRD and game design doc before building
- Realistic projectiles and hit reactions with the Rapier physics engine
- Seven weapons, each with its own projectile, sound and hit effect, plus rapid-fire mode
- Customizable, savable targets

## What I learned

I played both product manager and developer, from PRD to implementation, and learned how to handle 3D physics on the web.
